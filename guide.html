Tamagotchi P1

How to play

Feed it, keep it happy and clean, and it will grow up. Progress saves automatically.

  1. Set the clock: CONFIRM moves through the fields, SELECT changes the value.
  2. The egg hatches in about 5 minutes.
  3. Feed it: SELECT to the bowl icon, then CONFIRM (meal or snack).
  4. Care: turn the light off when it sleeps, clean up, give medicine when sick, scold it when it misbehaves.

The icons

Press SELECT to cycle through the icons, CONFIRM to choose, NEXT to cancel.
