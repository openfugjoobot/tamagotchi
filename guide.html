Tamagotchi P1

How to play

Your egg hatches in a few minutes. Feed it, play with it, tidy up after it — and watch it grow. It saves as you go.

  1. Set the clock. CONFIRM moves between the fields, SELECT changes the value.
  2. Wait for the hatch. The egg cracks open after about five minutes.
  3. Feed it. Pick the bowl with SELECT, then CONFIRM — a meal or a snack.
  4. Look after it. Lights off while it sleeps, a bath when it's messy, medicine when it's unwell, a gentle scold when it's naughty.

The menu

SELECT scrolls through it, CONFIRM picks, NEXT backs out.

Account & data

Playing needs no account: your pet is saved in this browser.
